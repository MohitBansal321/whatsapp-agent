# Potential Features for Bharat Loans AI Agent

Based on the current multi-tenant architecture and AI integration, here are several features that could make this platform even more powerful:

## 1. Automated Outbound WhatsApp Campaigns
- **Feature**: Instead of waiting for manual "Chat Now" clicks, allow organizations to schedule automated bulk outreach to leads using the WhatsApp Business API.
- **Value**: Massively scales the top of the funnel by engaging hundreds of leads simultaneously without human intervention.

## 2. Dynamic Lead Scoring
- **Feature**: Use the AI to analyze the conversation sentiment and explicit details (salary, loan amount, urgency) to automatically assign a "Lead Score" (1-100).
- **Value**: Helps human agents prioritize high-intent, high-value leads for manual follow-ups, improving overall conversion rates.

## 3. CRM Integration (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- **Feature**: Build two-way sync integrations with popular CRMs. When a lead is converted by the AI, their details and conversation history are automatically pushed to the CRM.
- **Value**: Reduces manual data entry and ensures this tool fits seamlessly into existing enterprise workflows.

## 4. Multi-Agent Handoff / Human-in-the-Loop
- **Feature**: An interface where human agents can silently monitor active AI conversations in real-time and "take over" the chat if the AI gets stuck or the user requests a human.
- **Value**: Provides a safety net for complex queries and ensures a smooth customer experience without frustrating dead-ends.

## 5. Voice Call AI Integration
- **Feature**: Expand beyond voice notes (which are currently supported) to actual live AI voice calls (e.g., using Twilio Voice + Gemini Multimodal Live API).
- **Value**: Addresses users who prefer traditional phone calls over text/WhatsApp, catering to a broader demographic in rural or semi-urban areas.

## 6. Advanced Analytics & Custom Dashboards
- **Feature**: Expand the current analytics tab to include custom date ranges, specific drop-off funnels, and exportable CSV reports for management.
- **Value**: Provides deeper actionable insights into campaign performance and AI effectiveness.
